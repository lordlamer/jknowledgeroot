Roles

RoleDoes
Incident leadDecides, keeps the timeline
CommunicatorUpdates the status page every 30 minutes
OperatorExecutes changes, nothing without the go of the lead

First 15 minutes

  1. Acknowledge the alert, open the incident channel
  2. Assign the three roles
  3. Post the first status update, even if it only says that we are investigating

Afterwards

A blameless review within three working days. The outcome is a list of actions with owners, not a list of mistakes.
