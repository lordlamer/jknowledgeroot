A review is a conversation about a change, not a gate. Reviewers answer three questions: does it do what the description says, could it break something that is not tested, and will we understand it in a year?

For authors

  • Describe why, not only what. Link the ticket.
  • Keep the change under roughly 400 lines; split otherwise.
  • Open a draft pull request early for design feedback.
  • Self-review the diff before requesting a review.

For reviewers

  • Respond within one working day, even if only to say when you will look.
  • Prefix comments: nit: for taste, question: for understanding, nothing for blocking issues.
  • Approve when it is good enough, not when it is how you would have written it.

Example of a blocking comment

This retries on every exception, including the ones that will never succeed
(invalid credentials). Please retry only on IOException and keep the limit.
